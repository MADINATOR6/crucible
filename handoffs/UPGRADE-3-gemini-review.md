## Executive Summary

UPGRADE-3's owned-path tamper exemption (`scripts/codex-dispatch.ps1:384, 580`; `scripts/crucible.ps1:293`) relies on the claim that *"Obsidian briefs still cannot own the launcher stack (`Test-Brief` unchanged)"* (`tasks/UPGRADE-3.md:15`, `scripts/crucible.ps1:292`, `AGENTS.md:126`). **That claim is refuted.** `Test-Brief` has **two independent bypasses** (backslash paths and comma smuggling) plus an existing-task ID gap that allow an Obsidian brief to own `scripts/ccx.ps1` or `scripts/`. Before UPGRADE-3, runtime denies and the unconditional protected-hash check still caught and blocked this. With UPGRADE-3, the brief lifts `write_file` denies on `scripts/`, passes both protected-hash checks with exit `0`, and `scripts/crucible.ps1:308-309` immediately executes the agent-edited `scripts/ccx.ps1` (or `scripts/check-sources.mjs` at line 304) outside any sandbox with zero approvals.

A second concrete defect (`scripts/codex-dispatch.ps1:621-622`) lets a timeout overwrite exit `9` with exit `5` after unowned protected files were modified, and real turbo execution (`agy --mode accept-edits --dangerously-skip-permissions --settings ...`) has never been tested live.
